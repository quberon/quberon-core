## What this proves

The three Wave 3/Wave 4 adversarial findings routed to the planner owner — non-finite values causing HTTP 500, oversized rejected-proposal echo, and trusted-looking provenance fields being silently ignored — no longer appear as escapes in this deterministic template corpus after PR #24. The endpoint did not invoke the adapter, change the latch, or obtain a secure-core decision through `/plan` in any checked result.

## What this does not prove

The corpus is still generated by deterministic programmatic templates. The templates and validator were authored by the same agent family, so this remains weak evidence against novel human attacks or an independent model adversary. It is not live-model evidence, not physical-robot evidence, and not a safety certification. The independent-model corpus remains blocked until a separately authorized application-provider run is performed under the evidence rules.
